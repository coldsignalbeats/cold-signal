Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026.

This uploader is a private, internal tool used only by its owner to upload and schedule the owner's own original videos to the owner's own YouTube channels through the YouTube API Services.

What it accesses: only the owner's own YouTube channels, to upload videos (as private with a scheduled publish time), set their titles, descriptions, thumbnails and captions, add them to the channel's own playlists, and list the channel's own uploads and playlists to avoid duplicates.

What it stores: sign-in tokens and a record of the owner's own uploaded video IDs, titles and publish times, kept only on the owner's own computer. No data about any other person, viewer or user is accessed, collected, stored, sold or shared.

Other users: the tool has no other users and is not offered to anyone else.

Google and YouTube: use of YouTube is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's Privacy Policy applies to data processed by Google.

Revoking access: access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact: open an issue on GitHub
