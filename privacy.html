Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026.

This uploader is a private tool used only by its owner to upload and schedule videos to the owner's own YouTube channels (Cold Signal, Standing Waves and Shape of Waves) through the YouTube Data API.

What it accesses: only the owner's own YouTube channels, to upload videos, set their titles, descriptions, thumbnails and publish times, and add them to the channel's playlists.

What it stores: a sign-in token kept on the owner's own computer. No data about any other person is collected, stored, sold or shared.

Other users: the tool is not offered to anyone else.

Revoking access: access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
