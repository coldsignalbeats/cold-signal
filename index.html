The uploader

Videos are published with a small private tool that the owner runs on their own computer. Through the YouTube API Services it uploads the owner's own original videos to the owner's own channels as private with a scheduled publish time, sets their thumbnails, captions and playlists, and lists the channel's own uploads to avoid duplicates. It has no other users and does not collect anyone else's data.

The uploader uses YouTube API Services. Use of it is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
