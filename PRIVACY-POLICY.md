This tool is a private, internal application used only by its owner to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channels through the YouTube API Services. It is not offered to, or used by, anyone else.

**Information accessed.** With the owner's permission (Google OAuth), the tool accesses only the owner's own YouTube channels to upload videos, set thumbnails and captions, manage the owner's own playlists, and list the owner's own uploads. It does not access, collect or store information about any other person, viewer or YouTube user.

**Storage.** The tool keeps a local record of the owner's own uploaded video IDs, titles and scheduled publish times to avoid duplicate uploads. OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's computer. No data is sold, shared or sent to any third party other than Google/YouTube as required to perform the uploads.
